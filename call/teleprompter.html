Beat 1 is a promise you have to keep. “I’ll already have looked at your site” means ten minutes of research per booked call — the cold campaign gives you zero personalization, so this line is what forces the homework that makes the call good. If you won’t reliably do it, cut the clause; don’t say it and skip it.

0–15s · Frame

Hey — Evan from Rushes. You booked a Growth Call. This isn’t a pitch dump. It’s a short roadmap session for your market — I’ll already have looked at your site and how leads come in today.

15–33s · Who I am

And so you’re not guessing about me — I’m not a big agency. I stay involved in the strategy, the creative and the system behind it. I’ll show you what we’ve actually built, and I’ll label what’s a controlled demo, what’s a concept and what’s a real client result. You’ll get that same straight answer about your business.

33–52s · Bring

Bring whatever you know off the top of your head: where the jobs come from now, what an average job is worth, how many more your crews could take on without hiring, and anything you’ve already tried that didn’t stick. Rough numbers are fine — I’d rather have your ballpark than a spreadsheet.

52–78s · Leave with

You’ll leave with a booked-estimate target built from your own numbers — not one I made up in an email — and a straight answer on whether the Demand Loop fits. That’s our system for turning the work you’ve already built into booked estimates on your calendar. If it’s not a fit, I’ll tell you that too.

78–85s · Close

I’ll send the Meet link again before we start. See you on the call.
