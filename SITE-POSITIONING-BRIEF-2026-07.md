## Execution plan (this pass)

**Goal:** An owner who lands on rushesmedia.com understands in one scroll: *what they get back* (booked estimates), *how* (the Demand Loop), *what to do* (Growth Call) — without sounding like an AI shop, ad shop, or “we post for you” media shop.

| Do | Don’t |
|----|-------|
| Keep “Media / done right.” + visual system | Redesign, new sections, motion theater |
| Promise = booked estimates | Lead with service menu or “lead-gen partner” |
| Name **Demand Loop** once (system section) | Lecture all 7 steps in the hero |
| Honest proof language | Fake ROAS guarantees / “booked jobs guaranteed” |
| CTA = Growth Call everywhere | Strategy Call · price dump · AI |

**Section map (copy only):**

1. **Meta / OG** — outcome line, not lead-gen partner  
2. **Hero** — keep mark · spine = Attention → Conversion → Compounding · subhead = booked estimates  
3. **Marquee** — demand/reach language, not “Paid Ads” as identity  
4. **Proof** — calendar / speed / partner (no fake ∞ theater rewrite — tighten labels)  
5. **Problem** — attention → booked estimate gap; kill unverified 3–4× claim  
6. **Services** — “Inside the Demand Loop” · four parts of one machine  
7. **System** — **The Demand Loop** · five connected steps · outcome = booked estimate  
8. **Process** — Growth Call → install → prove & compound  
9. **FAQ** — align prove-it + measure booked estimates · kill lead-gen partner line  
10. **Bottom CTA** — Growth Call · calendar fills language  

**Out of scope this pass:** niche funnel JSON, full visual redesign, Lane 4 ads, `/call` (already shipped).
