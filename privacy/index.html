SMS & text messaging

We send SMS only to individuals who opt in through our Growth Call booking form at https://rushesmedia.com/#book. After you select a date and time, the Enter details step includes two separate checkboxes (unchecked by default):

  • Customer care SMS — appointment confirmations, scheduling reminders, and follow-up related to your booking or inquiry.
  • Promotional SMS (optional) — special offers, service announcements, and marketing messages. This consent is separate from customer care and is not required to book.

Age restriction: You must be 18 years of age or older to opt in to SMS. Our SMS program is not directed to anyone under 18.

  • Message frequency varies.

  • Reply STOP to opt out at any time. Reply HELP for help.
