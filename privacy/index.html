Privacy Policy

Effective June 4, 2026  ·  Rushes Media, Haddon Heights, NJ

This Privacy Policy explains how Rushes Media (“we,” “us,” “our”) collects, uses, and protects the information you provide when you visit rushesmedia.com, book a call, complete a form, or communicate with us. By using our site or services, you agree to this policy.

Information we collect

  • Contact details you provide: name, business name, email address, and phone number.
  • Information about your business that you share during inquiries or calls.
  • Basic site analytics (pages visited, device and browser type) used to improve our site.

How we use your information

  • To respond to inquiries, schedule and confirm calls, and deliver our services.
  • To send appointment confirmations, reminders, and follow-up communications you've opted into.
  • To improve our website, offers, and client experience.

SMS & text messaging

When you provide your mobile number through our website form, by booking a call, or by replying to a message, and you consent via our opt-in checkbox, you agree to receive SMS text messages from Rushes Media related to appointment confirmations, reminders, and follow-ups.

  • Message frequency varies.
  • Message and data rates may apply.
  • Reply STOP at any time to opt out. Reply HELP for help.

We do not sell or share your information. Mobile information will not be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties under any circumstances.

How we share information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information with trusted service providers (such as our CRM and scheduling tools) solely to operate our business, and only to the extent needed. As stated above, SMS consent and mobile opt-in data are never shared with third parties.

Data retention & security

We retain information only as long as needed to provide our services and meet legal obligations, and we use reasonable safeguards to protect it.

Your choices

  • Opt out of SMS at any time by replying STOP.
  • Unsubscribe from emails using the link in any email.
  • Request access to or deletion of your information by emailing us.
